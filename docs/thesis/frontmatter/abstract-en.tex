\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This diploma thesis presents a complete optimization of the HQC post-quantum
key encapsulation mechanism on RISC-V, leveraging ratified ISA extensions including
Zbc (carry-less multiplication), Zvbc (vector carry-less multiplication), Zbb
(bit manipulation), and Zvbb (vector bit manipulation). At the HQC-128 level, the fully vectorized (Tier~3) implementation achieves a \speedup{296.3} speedup on polynomial multiplication and \speedup{154.1} on the full \KEM relative to the vectorized reference.

\begin{thesiskeywords}
  post-quantum cryptography,\; HQC,\; RISC-V,\; polynomial multiplication,\;
  Karatsuba,\; vector extensions,\; ISA optimization
\end{thesiskeywords}
