\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This diploma thesis presents an end-to-end optimization of the HQC post-quantum
key encapsulation mechanism on RISC-V, leveraging ratified ISA extensions including
Zbc (carry-less multiplication), Zvbc (vector carry-less multiplication), Zbb
(bit manipulation), and Zvbb (vector bit manipulation).

\vspace{1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Post-quantum cryptography, HQC, RISC-V, polynomial
multiplication, Karatsuba, vector extensions, ISA optimization
\end{english}
